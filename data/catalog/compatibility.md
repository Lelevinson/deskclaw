# Product Compatibility and Routine Guidance

Brand-authored guidance for combining and sequencing **DeskClaw's own products**. Use this file to answer "can I use X with Y?" and "what order do I apply these?" questions.

This file covers routine *ordering and pairing* of the products in [`products.json`](products.json) only. It is **not** medical, allergy, dermatological, or skin-condition advice. Products are referenced by their catalog name and id; their prices, stock, and descriptions live in `products.json`, not here.

Answer only from the facts written below. Do not invent pairings, ingredients, chemical interactions, effects, or routine steps that are not stated here.

## How to read this

- These are general routine suggestions for a typical skincare order, not personalized advice.
- "AM" means the morning routine; "PM" means the evening routine.
- The general step order is: cleanser, then toner, then moisturizer, then (AM) sunscreen or (PM) facial oil as the last step.
- If a customer asks about a product DeskClaw does not sell, or about anything in "Missing / Unsupported Details" below, do not answer from this file.

## Routine order

- A simple routine applies products from lightest to richest: cleanser first, then toner, then moisturizer, then the final step.
- In the AM, sunscreen is always the final step, applied after moisturizer.
- In the PM, a facial oil is the final step, applied after moisturizer.
- Cloud Cleanser (`cloud-cleanser`) is the first step in both the AM and PM routines.
- Sunny Shield SPF50 (`sunny-shield-spf50`) is a morning-only step and is always applied last in the AM routine.

## Safe pairings

- Clear Day Gel Moisturizer (`clear-day-gel`) and Calm Barrier Cream (`calm-barrier-cream`) can be used by the same person at different times of day: the lightweight gel in the AM and the richer cream in the PM.
- Calm Barrier Cream (`calm-barrier-cream`) and Night Repair Oil (`night-repair-oil`) can be layered in the PM: apply the cream first, then the oil as the last step to seal the routine.
- Cloud Cleanser (`cloud-cleanser`) is gentle enough to use before any other product in this catalog, in both the AM and PM.
- Sunny Shield SPF50 (`sunny-shield-spf50`) pairs with every AM routine and is especially recommended on mornings after using the Soft Reset Exfoliating Toner the night before.

## Use with care and avoid combining

- Soft Reset Exfoliating Toner (`soft-reset-toner`) is a PM-only step. Introduce it slowly — a few nights a week at first — rather than every night.
- Do not use the Soft Reset Exfoliating Toner (`soft-reset-toner`) and the Night Repair Oil (`night-repair-oil`) on the same night. Alternate them on different nights instead.
- After a night of using the Soft Reset Exfoliating Toner (`soft-reset-toner`), always apply Sunny Shield SPF50 (`sunny-shield-spf50`) the next morning.
- The Soft Reset Exfoliating Toner (`soft-reset-toner`) is intended for customers already comfortable with exfoliating products, not for a first beginner routine.
