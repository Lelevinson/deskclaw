# Product Care Policy

## General care

- Customers should follow the care label or product page instructions when they are available.
- Products should be kept in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
- Products should not be stored in a hot car, near a heater, or in a humid bathroom for long periods.

## Cleaning

- Reusable accessories may be wiped gently with a clean, soft cloth.
- Washable fabric items should be hand washed in cold water and air dried.
- Customers should not use bleach unless a product label specifically allows it.

## Use and safety

- Customers should stop using a product if it appears damaged.
- Customers should ask a human teammate if they are unsure whether a product is still safe to use.
- The available product-care policy does not provide medical, allergy, or skin-sensitivity advice.

## Storage after opening

- Items with lids or caps should be closed tightly after use.
- Products should be kept away from children and pets.
- The available product-care policy does not state a universal expiration date for every product.
