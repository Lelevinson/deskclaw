# Returns, Exchanges, and Refunds Policy

## Return window

- Customers may request a return within 7 days after the order is delivered.
- Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging.
- Customers should provide their order number when requesting a return.

## Exchanges

- Exchanges may be requested within 7 days after delivery.
- Exchanges are available only for unused, unwashed items in their original packaging.
- Exchanges depend on current stock availability.
- If the requested exchange item is unavailable, the customer may request a refund after the returned item is approved.

## Refunds

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item is received and approved.
- Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
- Original shipping fees are not refundable unless DeskClaw sent the wrong item or the item arrived defective.

## Return shipping

- For change-of-mind returns or exchanges, the customer pays return shipping.
- If DeskClaw sent the wrong item or the item arrived defective, DeskClaw covers the return shipping.

## Damaged, defective, or wrong items

- Customers should report a damaged, defective, or wrong item within 3 days after delivery.
- Customers should include their order number and clear photos of the issue.
- DeskClaw may offer a replacement, exchange, or refund after reviewing the issue.

## Final sale items

- Final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged unless they arrive defective or DeskClaw sent the wrong item.
